# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical focus.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and open to feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect differing materials-science and software backgrounds.
- Report errors and limitations honestly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation, and deliberate
misrepresentation of data or results are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may
remove comments, close contributions, or ban participants when necessary.
